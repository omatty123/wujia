How this practice works

People, concepts, and aphorisms practice associations in the course readings. Shared concepts can have more than one accepted house. Broad terms and modern applications are discussion cards, with no right/wrong score.

Aphorisms include paraphrases and story titles, not necessarily exact quotations. Supplementary material is identified in the feedback. This is self-study: nothing is submitted or added to your course grade.

Keyboard: Tab to a card, press Enter or Space, then Tab to a house button. Escape cancels selection. Progress stays in this browser when storage is available.
